Frequently asked questions

How long do projects take?

It depends on the type and size of the project; we set a clear schedule once we understand the requirements.

Do you work in Arabic and English?

Yes, websites, content and campaigns support Arabic and English (RTL/LTR).

How do you track campaign results?

Through GA4, conversion tracking and periodic reports showing performance and cost.

Do you build custom software and systems?

Yes, such as CRMs, dashboards and automation tools according to the client’s needs.

What is the difference between a website and a landing page?

A website has several pages that introduce a business, while a landing page is a single page built for one goal, such as collecting leads.

Which platforms do you run ads on?

We run Google Ads, Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok Ads campaigns.

Do you serve clients outside Egypt?

Yes, we work with clients in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Jordan and across the Arab world.
