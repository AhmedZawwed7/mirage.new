Google Ads Management in Saudi Arabia for Qualified Leads

MIRAGE manages Google Ads remotely for companies and service businesses in Saudi Arabia. Campaigns are planned around high-intent searches, real service coverage and the enquiries your sales team can follow up.

Discuss your requirements

Search campaigns built around buying intent

We separate commercial searches from research, jobs and training queries. Keyword themes, ad groups and landing pages are structured around the services you actually sell, then refined through search-term reviews and negative keywords.

Location targeting across Saudi cities

We assess whether Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam and other target areas need separate campaigns or can be managed together. The decision depends on search demand, budget, service coverage and differences in offer—not simply inserting city names into ads.

Landing pages and conversion measurement

A relevant ad needs a page that explains the service, establishes trust and makes the next step clear. Where access is available, we plan measurement for forms, calls and WhatsApp, and distinguish clicks from completed enquiries and qualified leads.

What we need before launch

We review priority services, ideal customer, target locations, monthly media budget, sales capacity and the process for qualifying enquiries. Media spend is separate from management fees; no fixed lead volume or cost per lead is guaranteed.

Keyword research for Saudi customer searches

Keyword selection starts with the service and the customer's next action. We separate searches for a provider, a specific service, a price or quote, and general information. Arabic and English search behaviour can differ by sector, so language, location modifiers and actual search terms are reviewed before budget is assigned. We also exclude irrelevant themes such as employment, courses and free downloads when they do not match the offer.

Campaign structure, ad relevance and budget control

Campaigns are grouped only when services, locations or budgets need different control. Ads explain the actual service, coverage and next step; they do not promise guaranteed rankings or lead volumes. Search terms, click quality, impression share and conversion data guide adjustments. Media spend is monitored separately from the agency management fee.

What counts as a useful conversion?

A WhatsApp button click is not the same as a completed conversation, and a form submission is not automatically a qualified sales opportunity. Where the client provides access, we can plan Google Tag Manager and GA4 events for calls, forms and WhatsApp, then compare the recorded actions with sales-team feedback. This helps optimise toward relevant enquiries instead of maximising low-value clicks.

Frequently asked questions

Do you manage Google Ads remotely for Saudi businesses?

Yes. MIRAGE manages campaigns remotely from Egypt for Saudi companies. Target locations, language, services and lead-handling arrangements are agreed before launch; MIRAGE does not claim to have a local Saudi office.

What monthly budget do I need for Google Ads in Saudi Arabia?

There is no universal minimum that suits every business. The budget depends on service demand, keyword competition, target cities, average click costs and the number of enquiries the business can handle. We recommend a test budget after reviewing these factors.

Are Google Ads spend and management fees separate?

Yes. The media budget paid to Google is separate from MIRAGE's campaign management fees, and both should be clear in the proposal.

Can you track calls, WhatsApp and website forms?

We can plan tracking for calls, WhatsApp actions and forms when the required website, Google Ads, GA4 or Google Tag Manager access is available. Events should be tested before they are used for optimisation.

Do you guarantee a fixed number of leads or a cost per lead?

No. Lead volume and cost depend on competition, demand, budget, offer, landing-page quality and sales follow-up. We optimise using campaign data and feedback on lead quality rather than promising a fixed result.
