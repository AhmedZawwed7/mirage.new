Google Ads Management in Saudi Arabia for Qualified Leads

MIRAGE manages Google Ads remotely for companies and service businesses in Saudi Arabia. Campaigns are planned around high-intent searches, real service coverage and the enquiries your sales team can follow up.

Discuss your requirements

Search campaigns built around buying intent

We separate commercial searches from research, jobs and training queries. Keyword themes, ad groups and landing pages are structured around the services you actually sell, then refined through search-term reviews and negative keywords.

Location targeting across Saudi cities

We assess whether Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam and other target areas need separate campaigns or can be managed together. The decision depends on search demand, budget, service coverage and differences in offer—not simply inserting city names into ads.

Landing pages and conversion measurement

A relevant ad needs a page that explains the service, establishes trust and makes the next step clear. Where access is available, we plan measurement for forms, calls and WhatsApp, and distinguish clicks from completed enquiries and qualified leads.

What we need before launch

We review priority services, ideal customer, target locations, monthly media budget, sales capacity and the process for qualifying enquiries. Media spend is separate from management fees; no fixed lead volume or cost per lead is guaranteed.
