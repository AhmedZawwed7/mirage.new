Google Ads Management in the UAE for Qualified Enquiries

MIRAGE works remotely with UAE companies and service businesses to plan and optimise Google Ads around commercial intent, the locations you serve and measurable business outcomes.

Discuss your requirements

Search campaigns for commercial intent

We organise keywords and ads around services customers are ready to enquire about. Search-term reviews help identify irrelevant traffic such as jobs, courses or free information when those are not your business goals; negative keywords are refined as data accumulates.

Emirate-level targeting with a business reason

Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and other Emirates may require different targeting when service availability, demand, budgets or sales processes differ. We avoid creating near-identical campaigns for every location without a measurable reason.

Landing pages and lead quality

Ad messaging should match the landing page, with clear service details and a useful next step. Where permissions allow, we plan tracking for forms, calls and WhatsApp, then use sales feedback to distinguish relevant enquiries from low-quality conversions.

Planning and budget transparency

Before launch, we confirm priority services, audience, locations served, language needs, monthly media budget and lead follow-up process. Ad spend is separate from management fees, and results depend on competition, offer, website and sales response.

Keyword research in Arabic and English

Search behaviour in the UAE can vary by service, language and customer segment. We review Arabic and English keyword themes, commercial modifiers such as provider, service, price and quotation, and the locations a business can genuinely serve. Search terms are checked after launch to identify irrelevant intent, including jobs, courses and free resources where those are not relevant.

Campaign structure across Emirates

Separate campaigns are useful when an Emirate has a different offer, budget, service capacity or conversion performance. Otherwise, combining locations may provide clearer budget control and enough data to optimise. We use actual service coverage and campaign evidence rather than creating duplicate campaigns simply to repeat city names.

Conversion tracking and lead qualification

Where website and advertising access is available, measurement can cover calls, enquiry forms and WhatsApp actions through Google Tag Manager and GA4. A click is recorded as an interaction, not automatically as a qualified lead. Sales feedback helps distinguish genuine prospects from accidental clicks, spam and enquiries outside the service area.
