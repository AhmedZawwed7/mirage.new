Social Media Marketing in the UAE for Growing Brands

MIRAGE supports businesses in the UAE with remote social media planning and management, creating a consistent presence that reflects the brand, its audience and the actions that matter to the business.

Discuss your requirements

Content for a multilingual audience

We plan Arabic and English content when both languages serve real audience segments. Copy is adapted for meaning and customer context rather than translated word for word, while keeping the brand promise and service details consistent.

Platform strategy and monthly calendar

We select platforms and formats based on the audience and the organisation's capacity to respond. A calendar can define topics, post types, stories, short videos, publishing dates and approval owners for a predictable workflow.

Creative production and enquiry handling

Depending on the agreed scope, work can include captions, visual design, short-form video concepts, scheduling and account coordination. We agree how comments and direct messages are handled so interested prospects receive a timely response.

Measure useful outcomes

Reporting can cover reach, engagement, video views, profile actions, clicks and enquiries. We look at these metrics against the purpose of each content theme; follower growth alone is not treated as proof of business impact.
